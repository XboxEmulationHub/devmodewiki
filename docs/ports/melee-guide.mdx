Melee UWP `0.4.2.0` brings Melee to Xbox Dev Mode with online play, memory cards, texture packs, and in-game settings.

### Requirements
- **Super Smash Bros. Melee USA, revision 2 (NTSC-U 1.02)** disc image. None is included, so you must supply your own.
- The required game ID is `GALE01`.
- Supported image formats: `.iso`, `.gcm`, `.ciso`, `.rvz`, `.gcz`, and `.wia`.
- Extract ZIP files before selecting an image.

### Install the Melee UWP App
- Install the Melee `.appx` package via the Xbox Device Portal.
- Install the `Microsoft.VCLibs` dependency file.
- Launch **Melee UWP** from Dev Mode.

### Internal Storage Setup
- Place your game image in:

```text
D:\DevelopmentFiles\Melee
```

- Launch the app.
- The app creates its folders there on first launch.

### External Storage Setup
- Launch the app.
- In **Choose Disc**, select your external drive.
- The app creates a `Melee` folder on that drive for saves, settings, music, and texture packs.

For example, if your drive is `E:`, the folder is:

```text
E:\Melee
```

The cache is always stored on the internal drive, even when playing from USB:

```text
D:\DevelopmentFiles\Melee\cache
```

### Texture Packs
- Extract Dolphin-format `tex1_*.png` or `tex1_*.dds` packs into the `textures` folder on your selected drive.
- Keep the pack's subfolders, including `GALE01` if present.

Internal:

```text
D:\DevelopmentFiles\Melee\textures
```

USB (if your drive is `E:`):

```text
E:\Melee\textures
```

To reload files after copying them, turn **Custom HD textures** off and back on in:

```text
Settings > Graphics
```

### In-Game Settings
Press **Start + Select** together to open or close the menu.

| Xbox Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad | Navigate |
| A | Select |
| B | Go back |
| Start + Select | Open or close the menu |

Graphics, audio, controls, and online options are available from the menu.

### Direct Connect
Both players need the same build and game image. Works with both Xbox and PC players.

Open:

```text
VS Mode > ONLINE > DIRECT CONNECT
```

- One player chooses **Wait for a friend to call me** and presses **Y** to copy their connect code.
- The other player chooses **Enter a friend's code**, enters the eight characters after `#`, and presses **Start** to call.

Clipboard codes and recent opponents can also be selected from the Direct Connect screen. Some networks may not support a direct connection.
