### Requirements
- **Unmodified USA ISO** of the game.
- The required game ID is `SLUS-21678`.
- Optional: a **European ISO** (`SLES-54945`) adds Spanish, German, French, and Italian.

### Install the Tenkaichi3Decomp UWP App
- Install `bt3.msix` via the Xbox Device Portal.
- Install the included x64 VCLibs dependency file.
- Launch the app from Dev Mode.

### Internal Storage Setup
- Place your ISO in:

```text
D:\DevelopmentFiles\BT3
```

- Launch the app and select your ISO on first launch.
- Choose **Internal**, then **Install and Play**.
- Game data extracts to `D:\DevelopmentFiles\BT3`.

### External Storage Setup
- Launch the app and select the ISO on your external drive, usually `E:\`.
- Choose **External**, then **Install and Play**.
- Game data extracts to:

```text
E:\BT3
```

### Storage Notes
- The ISO can be removed after extraction.
- Keep the external drive connected to play when using external storage.
- Saves and settings stay in `D:\DevelopmentFiles\BT3` with either setup.

### Online Play
- Host or join using room and lobby codes, or use a direct address and port.
- Codes use upstream's public code service.
- PC lobbies and spectators require a compatible PC build, version `0.1.38.8` or newer.
- Quick Match may reject unofficial builds.

### Texture Packs
- DDS and PNG textures are supported, including subfolders.
- Place packs in the `textures` folder on your selected drive.

Internal:

```text
D:\DevelopmentFiles\BT3\textures
```

External (if your drive is `E:`):

```text
E:\BT3\textures
```

- The internal texture folder takes priority when both exist.
- Large packs may affect frame rate.
